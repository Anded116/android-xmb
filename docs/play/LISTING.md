## English

**Short description** (≤ 80):
> A console-style home screen: sections across, apps down. Fast, gamepad-ready.

**Full description:**

[NAME] turns your home screen into a cross menu: sections run across the screen, apps run down. Swipe to change
the section, tap any app to open it — no grids, no pages, no clutter.

**Made for phones and handhelds**
• Full gamepad and keyboard control: D-pad, A to open, X for the app menu, Y for search, L1/R1 to page
• Works in portrait and landscape, scales to tablets, TVs and small handheld screens
• Games section picks up emulators and game frontends automatically

**Smart, not busy**
• Apps sorted into sections automatically: Social, Games, Entertainment, Finance, Travel and more; empty ones hide
• «Frequent» learns what you use; optional dock for your five favourites
• Search with typo tolerance, transliteration and wrong-keyboard-layout detection
• App shortcuts (recent chats, actions) right in the long-press menu and in search

**Looks**
• Three looks: Classic wave, Dot matrix and Aurora; twelve colour themes
• White silhouette icons, free-form colour icons or any ADW/Nova icon pack; custom icon shapes
• Live wallpaper so the background stays in place in recents
• Notification badges and a «Now» card with music controls and your next event (optional)

**Private by design**
No internet permission, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on your device.

другой раздел, тап по любому приложению — запуск. Без сеток, страниц и лишнего.
